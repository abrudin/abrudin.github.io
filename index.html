02 / CREATE

Two tracks. Endless possibilities.

V/B arms or stops recording and starts the metronome immediately. The first note starts recording from the beginning of its bar, filling earlier beats with rests. Escape cancels and restores the previous line.

Separate notes with spaces (c4 d4); use r for a rest and commas for a chord (c4,e4,g4). Starting playback from the beginning pads the shorter line with rests so both finish together.
