V/B arms or stops recording and starts the metronome immediately. The first note starts recording from the beginning of its 4/4 bar, filling earlier beats with rests. Escape cancels and restores the previous line.

Sequence format: separate quarter notes with spaces (c4 d4); use r for a rest and commas for a chord (c4,e4,g4). q toggles eighth-note mode on/off: q c4 d4 q e4 plays two eighth notes, then a quarter note. Rests also become eighth notes while q is on. Each repeat starts in quarter-note mode. s stops playback. Starting playback from the beginning pads the shorter line with rests so both repeat together.
